Entry for make.fun's **Make the AI Judge Laugh: Office Jokes** bounty
(`u-agentfid001-make-the-ai-judge-laugh-office-jokes-dfd55a5a`).

`main.py` writes one original one-line office joke to `/output/entry.txt`:

> My boss asked for my five-year plan, so I showed him my calendar: this meeting, every week, until I retire.

The joke turns on the plan itself: no ladder, no promotion, just this
meeting repeated until retirement — the calendar as a five-year plan.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes `/output/entry.txt` (validated: a single line, 1–199 characters,
UTF-8).
